How these results were scored

Your answers were mapped to ten broad IT families using a transparent, weighted model — no black box. Each family is shown as a band, never a percentage, because the underlying question types aren't precise enough to justify a number like “87% fit.” Two paths a few points apart aren't meaningfully different, so we cluster them rather than false-rank.

No single question can decide a result. Each answer moves its family only a little, and a “Strong fit” additionally requires several independent answers to point the same way. Interest and confidence are measured separately — feeling unsure about a path you're drawn to lands it in “Worth exploring” with a trial to try, never removes it.

Cybersecurity is never your #1 first job. It's real and well-paid, but almost always reached after a support, networking, or systems rung — so we show it as a destination with the on-ramp named, per U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics guidance that these roles typically need prior experience.

Salary and growth figures are U.S. BLS national medians (May 2024 / 2024–34 projections) — a median is the midpoint of everyone employed, so entry pay runs lower; treat every figure as directional and verify against the live source before making decisions.

This is a starting point, not a definitive answer. It maps how you like to work to fields worth exploring — it can't tell you your “perfect career,” guarantee a job, or replace talking to people who do the work. The best next step is the weekend trial: the goal is to discover what you enjoy by doing, then validate from there.
